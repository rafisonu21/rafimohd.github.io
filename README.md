# Parallel You

**Parallel You** is a web application that lets you explore alternate versions of your life based on different choices you could have made. Ever wonder what life would be like if you'd pursued a different career, lived in another city, or followed a hidden passion? This app brings those "what ifs" to life.

## How It Works

1.  **Answer a Few Prompts:** Share your current job or degree, city, a hobby, and a choice you've always wondered about.
2.  **Generate Your Parallel Self:** Our (placeholder) AI logic creates a "Parallel Life Profile" with a new persona, lifestyle, and a short story about your alternate self.
3.  **Discover and Share:** Each profile is presented as a futuristic, shareable trading card.

## Features

-   **Dynamic Profile Generation:** See different outcomes based on your inputs.
-   **Shareable Cards:** Easily share your "Parallel You" with friends on social media.
-   **Addictive Triggers:** Come back to explore more timelines and possibilities.

## Visual Style

The website features a minimal, surreal design with dreamlike gradients and a floating "mirror" effect to symbolize alternate realities. Each generated profile is styled as a futuristic trading card, creating a unique and engaging user experience.
